    Simple process. Zero guesswork.

    01 Discovery

    We start with a conversation. We learn about your brand, your goals, your audience, and what you need from your website. No jargon, no complicated forms, just a real chat.

    02 Proposal & Quote

    Based on what we discussed, we put together a clear proposal outlining exactly what we'll build, the timeline, and the cost. No hidden fees, no surprises.

    03 Design Phase

    We begin designing your website: layouts, colours, typography, and the overall feel. You'll see it take shape and have the chance to give feedback before a single line of code is written.

    04 Development

    Once the design is approved, we build it. Every interaction, animation, and page, coded cleanly and precisely.

    05 Review & Revisions

    We walk you through the finished build and make any final adjustments. This is your chance to refine anything before it goes live.

    06 Launch & Handover

    We launch your website and hand everything over to you: files, access, the works. And if you need anything after, we're still here.
